# Contract A RC2 → Contract E Pre-Promotion Pipeline Gate

Status: **PREREGISTERED / NOT YET EXECUTED**

Class: Research / pre-promotion falsification.

Promotion authority: EDR-004 / #60.

This experiment is a required gate before any Contract A production promotion. It does not promote, merge, release, tag, or assign canonical production authority to Contract A RC2 or Contract E.

## 1. Scientific question

Can the exact frozen Contract A RC2 declaration/root/decomposition/source authority survive the legitimate pinned pipeline into the bounded Contract E authority layer without:

- proposition minting;
- root/atom lineage loss;
- identity substitution or replay;
- stale-binding acceptance;
- semantic-authority laundering from fields outside Contract A;
- treating Contract A producer/declaration identity as an authorization grant, policy, delegation, jurisdiction, or execution authority?

The objective is falsification, not a ceremonial end-to-end pass.

## 2. Primary hypothesis

`H1`: the exact frozen Contract A RC2 authority boundary is preserved through the pinned pipeline, and Contract E authority can become conferring only from an independent explicit authority basis rather than from Contract A declaration identity or excluded upstream metadata.

## 3. Null / failure hypotheses

Any of the following is material evidence against the promotion gate:

- `H0-A1`: a legitimate downstream consumer loses, changes, or re-mints an A-authoritative root/child proposition identity or text while still treating it as the same lineage;
- `H0-A2`: child/root/source/work/handoff substitution or replay can survive a stale or forged Contract A binding;
- `H0-A3`: support/confidence/trust/retrieval/model/workflow/history fields excluded from A authority can change downstream proposition identity or Contract E authority;
- `H0-E1`: Contract A producer identity, handoff existence, decomposition declaration, or supplied-source identity alone is sufficient to create a conferring Contract E authority basis;
- `H0-PIPE1`: the full legitimate pipeline cannot carry the frozen A authority without an adapter that adds semantic defaults, rewrites identities, invents propositions, or imports Contract E semantics upstream;
- `H0-EVAL1`: the chosen evaluator/oracle is non-discriminating, internally inconsistent, or cannot separate an A defect from an E/harness defect.

## 4. Load-bearing assumptions

1. Exact frozen Git objects are the scientific subjects. A changed A object is a successor, not the same candidate.
2. Representation adapters may change serialization/call shape but may not mint authority, add semantic defaults, alter authoritative IDs/text/hashes/order, or infer missing state.
3. Contract E is a downstream authorization/authority-control-plane layer. Contract A is not.
4. The bounded Contract E RC0B authority-chain evaluator is usable only for the properties it actually demonstrated. It is not a universal Contract E oracle.
5. Known unresolved Contract E semantics must not be silently resolved to make this experiment scoreable.
6. One complete authority-conferring record is sufficient for primary positive controls only where that rule is already supported. Surplus-record behavior is excluded from primary scoring.

## 8. Contract E unresolved-semantics exclusion boundary

### Qualification binding

PR #58 is terminal `QUALIFICATION_BINDING_UNDERDETERMINED`.

The surviving Contract E authority does not uniquely determine subject/scope qualification matching. Historical exact equality is not promoted as normative authority.

Therefore primary scored cases MUST avoid requiring a choice among competing qualification subject/scope predicates. Qualification-focused cases may be exploratory only and must be reported `UNDERDETERMINED` / excluded from primary A promotion scoring.

### Surplus authority-basis aggregation

PR #59 is terminal `PARTIAL_AGGREGATION_CLOSURE`.

Supported result: one satisfying authority basis must contain at least one single resolved authority record whose own bounds satisfy all applicable RC3B predicates. Cross-record partial union is unsupported.

Unresolved result: the effect of surplus/multiple conferring records after one complete match.

Therefore primary positive cases MUST use exactly one complete conferring authority record. Multiple-conferring-record cases are exploratory and excluded from primary A promotion scoring unless a later frozen authority closes that rule before implementation freeze, in which case changing the aperture requires a successor preregistration.

## 9. Experimental lanes

### Lane A: Contract A integrity and proposition lineage

For each A state (`not_decomposed`, `failed`, `unknown`, declared `all_of`):

- validate the exact frozen A object;
- materialize the exact downstream retrieval proposition target(s);
- record root and atom IDs, exact text, `text_sha256`, sequence/order, work ID, source IDs/content hashes, and A `handoff_sha256`;
- prove that each later stage either preserves those values directly or carries an explicit immutable reference sufficient to reconstruct them without guessing.

Primary failure: any legitimate stage silently substitutes, drops, or invents an authoritative proposition while still claiming same lineage.

### Lane B: Pinned producer/consumer pipeline participation

Exercise the real pinned production/released code path wherever a public/native interface exists:

A RC2 → Evidence Bundler / Contract B → Claim Audit Lab → Contract C → Decision Engine / Contract D → bounded Contract E adapter/oracle.

For each stage record:

- exact repository SHA;
- exact module/function/command invoked;
- input artifact identity/hash;
- output artifact identity/hash;
- proposition/source/lineage fields consumed and emitted;
- any representation-only adapter and its exact bytes.

A stage that cannot natively consume the immediately upstream representation is evidence. A mechanical adapter is allowed only if it does not add semantic behavior.

### Lane C: Authority non-laundering

Construct matched pairs that differ only in fields outside Contract A semantic authority, including where representable:

- support/confidence labels;
- retrieval query/rank/history;
- trust/source heuristics;
- model/prompt/config metadata;
- workflow state;
- timestamps/history;
- selected-passage scaffolding.

Required outcome: those mutations may affect legitimate owners downstream only where their own contract grants that authority, but they MUST NOT become Contract E grant/policy/delegation authority merely because they traveled with an A-derived object.

### Lane D: Contract E positive/negative authority controls

Primary positive cases provide exactly one complete, explicit, independently supplied E authority-conferring record whose required non-qualification predicates match the request.

Primary negative controls include:

- no authority basis;
- explicit non-conferring basis;
- mismatched participant/request binding where already normatively defined;
- stale or forged authority-chain lineage;
- A producer identity substituted for grantor/policy/delegation authority;
- A `handoff_id`/`handoff_sha256` present with no independent conferring E basis;
- valid A declaration with invalid/missing E basis;
- invalid A stale binding paired with otherwise valid E material.

Expected distinction: A can establish declaration lineage; only E-owned authority material can establish E authority.

### Lane E: substitution, replay, and resealing controls

At minimum test:

- root identity substitution;
- child identity substitution;
- source identity substitution;
- work identity substitution;
- handoff identity substitution;
- stale A whole-object binding;
- legitimate A bound-field change plus fresh reseal as a new declaration identity;
- replay of a previously valid A reference into a different downstream request;
- forged linkage claiming a different A handoff hash.

The evaluator must distinguish stale/forged lineage from a legitimately resealed new declaration. A fresh reseal does not mean the new declaration inherits old downstream authority automatically.

## 10. Required mutation/metamorphic properties

1. Changing an A bound field without resealing must fail before downstream authority use.
2. Legitimately resealing an A bound-field change creates a different valid declaration, not continuity with the old downstream E authority unless E separately authorizes it.
3. Reordering declared A atoms where sequence is authoritative must not silently preserve the same A binding.
4. Changing excluded A metadata in a compatibility carrier must not change A proposition/source-contract semantic identity.
5. Removing the independent E conferring basis from an otherwise identical pipeline case must remove E conferring authority.
6. Adding a valid A producer/work/source declaration to an E-negative case must not convert it into an E-positive case.
7. Adding one complete independent E conferring basis to an otherwise valid A pipeline case may convert the E result only according to frozen E authority rules.
8. A mutation that only touches qualification subject/scope matching must not be used to score A because that E predicate is underdetermined.
9. A mutation that only adds a second complete conferring E record must not be used to score A because surplus aggregation is unresolved.

## 11. Evaluator self-test and independence controls

Before interpreting a pipeline result:

1. run the exact frozen E evaluator against its exact frozen RC0B candidate/held-out corpus;
2. verify expected case count and recorded integrity identities where available;
3. preserve any evaluator failure as `EVALUATOR_DEFECT` or `INCONCLUSIVE`, not an A failure;
4. run the new A→E harness against explicit positive and negative controls that would fail if the harness always returned one class;
5. ensure the A object and E oracle bytes are frozen before final evaluation;
6. record any invocation adapter separately from both frozen subjects.

No evaluator may be repaired after seeing a target result and then counted as independent evidence for the original run. A repaired evaluator is a successor.

## 13. Primary gate decision rule

### `SUPPORTED FOR PROMOTION`

Allowed only if all primary scored cases show:

- exact frozen A validity/integrity behavior preserved;
- no authoritative root/atom/source/work/handoff lineage loss or silent substitution;
- no excluded A field acquires Contract E authority;
- A declaration/producer identity alone never creates a conferring E basis;
- exactly-one-complete-basis E-positive controls confer and matched E-negative controls do not;
- no in-scope unresolved A disagreement remains;
- any E underdetermination is isolated to preregistered excluded predicates and cannot explain the primary A result;
- evaluator self-tests and discriminating controls succeed.

### `FALSIFIED`

Use if a reproducible in-scope defect is attributable to the frozen Contract A RC2 authority boundary or if the legitimate pipeline requires A to own semantics that RC2 explicitly removed.

Do not repair RC2 in place. Preserve the frozen result and create the smallest successor experiment.

### `INCONCLUSIVE`

Use if the primary gate cannot be discriminated because of Contract E underdetermination, a pipeline/evaluator defect, unavailable exact authority, or another blocker not attributable to Contract A.

Do not promote on an inconclusive gate.

## 14. Promotion hold

Even a `SUPPORTED FOR PROMOTION` result from this experiment does not itself merge Contract A.

If the gate is supported, the next step is a separate minimal production PR that transcribes the surviving frozen RC2 semantic surface into proposed canonical Contract A `2.0.0` naming/layout. That transcription must have a mechanical semantic-equivalence gate back to the frozen RC2 authority before merge.

The production PR must not absorb Contract E fields or unresolved Contract E semantics.

## 15. Alternatives considered

### Immediate Contract A promotion

Rejected for this sequence. Independent recovery establishes recoverability, not full downstream authority-boundary composition. The A→E test is a smaller and cheaper falsifier before a breaking production migration.

### Wait for universal Contract E closure

Rejected. It would couple Contract A promotion to unresolved E predicates that Contract A does not own. The primary experiment can avoid those predicates while still testing authority laundering and lineage preservation.

### Add E authority fields to A before testing

Rejected because it pre-answers the boundary question and violates the currently supported ownership split.

### Test only A→CAL and infer the rest

Rejected. Existing A evidence already reaches CAL. The new information is whether the frozen A lineage remains bounded when passed through the actual downstream decision/authority pipeline.

## 16. Freeze rule

This preregistration is part of the experiment authority. Once committed, changing its scientific question, primary scoring boundary, frozen A identity, pipeline pins, E oracle identities, or exclusion rules requires a visibly named successor preregistration.

Implementation/harness code may be added after this preregistration freeze, but must not silently broaden the scientific aperture or change the primary decision rule.

- qualification subject/scope matching;
- surplus/multiple-conferring-record aggregation;
- CAL NLI accuracy;
- Evidence Bundler retrieval completeness or ranking quality;
- source truth/authenticity/trust beyond supplied identity and bytes;
- decomposition semantic correctness;
- proposition truth/support/refutation;
- general Decision Engine correctness beyond the exercised bounded path;
- operational authorization execution or autonomous mutation;
- Contract A production `2.0.0` until a separate promotion/equivalence gate completes.
