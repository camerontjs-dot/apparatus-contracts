## Research question

Use a deliberately richer companion attribution vessel to determine which information is genuinely necessary for legitimate downstream capabilities, which information is integrity scaffolding, which information is conditional/recoverable from Contract B or Contract C, and which information is redundant.

The vessel was not treated as an equally authorized canonical architecture. EDR-002 currently requires retained neutral/non-deciding provenance to be part of Contract C's semantic obligation.

## Tested capabilities

The experiment separately pressured:

1. bounded destination policy behavior;
2. exact attribution reconstruction;
3. causal multiplicity reconstruction;
4. replay/substitution/integrity behavior;
5. structured audit/explanation reconstruction.

Positive shapes included single causal neutral evidence, independently sufficient neutral alternatives, jointly sufficient neutral members, supported plus neutral residual evidence, residual-only neutral evidence, a multi-state carrier, and a released supported control.

## Preregistered expectation falsification

The frozen expectation that a separate sidecar `member_id` was `REQUIRED_INTEGRITY` was **falsified**.

Across the tested capabilities, exact evidence references plus exact carrier/object binding were sufficient. Removing the additional member identifier did not impair semantic reconstruction or the tested integrity guarantees. This result is preserved rather than rationalized away.

This does not imply that released Contract C's existing `contribution_id` should be removed. It establishes only that the sidecar did not justify a second independent member-identity layer.

## Metamorphic result

Two vessels with the exact same evidence members but different causal forms, `independent_sufficient_alternatives` versus `jointly_sufficient`, remained materially distinct only because causal form was preserved.

Removing causal form collapsed those two different epistemic structures to the same observable member set. This supports causal form as a semantic invariant rather than display metadata.

Likewise, a mixed causal/residual case demonstrated that role classification is required to reconstruct why evidence participated or did not participate in the terminal basis.

## In-band versus sidecar finding

The sidecar is **supported as a research vessel**. It successfully exposed necessity, redundancy, conditional recoverability, replay/binding behavior, and causal multiplicity.

However, the experiment does not support making the sidecar canonical under current authority.

The reason is structural:

- released Contract C already contains proposition binding, exact evidence-reference structure, causal-versus-residual placement, causal form, result-set identity, whole-object handoff binding, and Contract-B binding;
- the sidecar had to duplicate/rebind those concepts and add conditional state binding plus its own separate object-authorization problem;
- the already-qualified in-band research candidate adds the missing neutral semantic category through `non_deciding` while reusing those existing Contract C structures.

Within the tested scope, the **smallest new in-band semantic delta remains the `non_deciding` contribution channel**. The research does not justify adding sidecar-specific `member_id`, state binding, carrier rebinding, or a second receipt identity to Contract C merely because the sidecar needed them.

## What this supports

Supported, bounded inference:

1. neutral/non-deciding evidence membership is required for deep downstream reconstruction;
2. causal versus residual role and causal multiplicity are real semantic invariants;
3. those invariants can be carried by the existing Contract C contribution/basis/causal-form architecture once neutral evidence is representable;
4. some sidecar fields are conditional or redundant because Contract C and Contract B already carry the underlying identities;
5. a companion vessel remains valuable for future research and ablation without committing the production wire;
6. current EDR-002 aligns more naturally with an in-band Contract C successor than with making a mandatory sidecar canonical.

## What remains unestablished

This experiment does **not** establish:

- an official Contract C successor version;
- that the exact PR #85 JSON schema is the final successor schema;
- migration or compatibility policy beyond the already-established breaking-change signal;
- independent downstream recovery of the minimized invariant set from a fresh consumer;
- maintained Decision Engine support for the successor;
- cryptographic origin authentication;
- arbitrary future CAL semantic-family coverage;
- release, merge, promotion, operational Authorization, or execution authority.

## Highest-value successor

Freeze the minimized in-band invariant set as a static handoff and give it to a separate Decision Engine research consumer from the current protected Decision baseline. Require that consumer to independently recover exact neutral evidence membership, causal/residual role and causal form, preserve existing supported-claim policy behavior, reject downgrade/laundering and hostile binding mutations, and demonstrate that no sidecar-specific redundant fields are needed.

Only after that cross-repository consumer pressure should the programme decide whether there is enough evidence to draft a concrete Contract C successor/promotion candidate.
