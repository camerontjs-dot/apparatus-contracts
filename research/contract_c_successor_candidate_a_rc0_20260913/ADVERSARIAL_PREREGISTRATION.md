# Candidate A RC0 adversarial qualification preregistration

**Classification:** Draft Research / Research Infrastructure.

**Frozen candidate under test:** `9f1808c47503c884887dbf862e9cd8162b21b583`.

This preregistration is written after candidate freeze and before the decisive evaluator/result is added. The candidate files may not change after attack reveal. A needed candidate repair falsifies RC0 and creates a successor experiment.

## Qualification question

Does the exact frozen Candidate A RC0 preserve the Phase 0–2 public obligations, canonicalize set-like structure deterministically, reject structurally incoherent or out-of-bound state, and make adversarial semantic substitutions observably different/fail closed under the complete authority stack strongly enough to justify a separate exact-CAL producer-conformance experiment?

A PASS does **not** mean that Contract C bytes prove causal truth. Causal minimality against real CAL state remains a producer-conformance obligation. This cohort asks whether the frozen representation can carry the truth without false equivalence and whether the verifier stack detects the preregistered attack classes.

## Frozen positive cohort

Re-encode and validate the same 19 Phase 2 SP/EX specimens from the frozen oracle:

`SP-01` through `SP-14`, and `EX-01` through `EX-05`.

Required special cases include independent support/refute alternatives, joint non-polarized basis, neutral residual, causal unresolved neutral, mixed support/refute, CAL #106 alternative-joint, its symmetric counterpart, representation-stress alt-by-alt, result-set multi-proposition, failed/incomplete execution, and completed `not_checkable`.

Every positive specimen must:

1. validate structurally/semantically under the frozen RC0 validator;
2. reconstruct the expected public participant relations/roles and basis-group family exactly;
3. resolve every participant against the exact bound Contract-B index;
4. resolve the exact implementation/policy pair through the independently selected immutable Phase 1.5 resolver authority;
5. preserve its independently recorded full-object handoff digest.

## Canonical equality controls

Pure permutations of semantically unordered structures must have the same canonical bytes, local `result_set_id`, and whole-object digest after normalization. Exercise at least:

- participant order;
- basis-group order;
- member order within a joint group;
- proposition order in the multi-proposition specimen;
- combined permutation of all above.

Any permutation changing semantic identity falsifies RC0.

## Seeded weak controls

The evaluator must prove sensitivity, not merely report all green. Seed at least three intentionally weak comparators/consumers:

1. a local-reseal-only verifier that trusts a recomputed object ID and omits independent handoff authority; it must be shown vulnerable to coherent semantic deletion;
2. a neutral-laundering consumer that treats `non_polarized` causal evidence as support; it must be shown to produce an unsafe strengthening on the unresolved specimen;
3. a basis-shape checker that accepts any covering joint group without comparing against the frozen minimal-basis oracle; it must be shown vulnerable to the fake-joint attack on independent alternatives.

If the harness cannot distinguish these weak controls from RC0's required verification stack, the experiment is insensitive and must not qualify the candidate.

## Acceptance rule

Disposition is `QUALIFIED_FOR_CAL_PRODUCER_CONFORMANCE_RESEARCH` only if all of the following hold with the frozen candidate bytes unchanged:

- 19/19 positive SP/EX specimens pass and reconstruct exactly;
- all canonical permutation controls preserve one canonical identity;
- 36/36 preregistered attacks are detected by the appropriate gate or become an explicitly different oracle/object identity rather than silently equivalent state;
- all three weak controls are demonstrably vulnerable while the required RC0 stack catches the corresponding attack;
- no attack produces a false causal equivalence, false unique winner, neutral laundering, execution/epistemic laundering, cross-world replay, producer/policy substitution, or boundary injection that remains valid under the full required verifier stack;
- `apparatus_failures=[]` and no post-freeze candidate repair is required.

Otherwise the terminal disposition is `FALSIFIED_REQUIRES_SUCCESSOR_CANDIDATE` or `INCONCLUSIVE_EXPERIMENT_INSENSITIVE`, whichever matches the failure class.
