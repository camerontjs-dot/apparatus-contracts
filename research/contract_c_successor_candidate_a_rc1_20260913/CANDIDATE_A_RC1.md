## Causal semantics

`basis_groups` is the canonical family of minimal sufficient causal participant sets for one proposition terminal result. Examples:

```text
single:                    {{S1}}
independent alternatives:  {{S1},{S2}}
joint:                     {{S1,S2}}
mixed:                     {{S1,R1}}
alternative-joint:         {{S1,R1},{S2,R1}}
```

Normative structural rules:

1. every group is non-empty;
2. every member is a unique exact retained participant reference;
3. every group member has role `causal`;
4. equivalent groups are unique;
5. no represented group is a strict superset of another represented group;
6. the union of all groups is exactly the set of causal participants;
7. residual participants never enter a basis group.

For the frozen scope, supported bases are support-only, contradicted bases are refute-only, `MIXED_RELATIONS` bases contain support and refutation, unresolved/joint-public-cause bases are non-polarized, and `no_deciding_relation` has no causal basis and may retain only residual non-polarized participants.

These rules establish canonical minimal normal form inside the claimed family. Candidate bytes cannot prove the producer's empirical claim that a claimed basis is truly minimal. Exact comparison to the frozen oracle is an adversarial-qualification obligation, and later CAL producer conformance must derive the family from legitimate CAL authority/ablation state without a new epistemic judgment.
