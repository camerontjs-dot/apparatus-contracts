## What this release establishes

Contract D 1.0.0 establishes a deterministic, exact-version Decision authority object with:

- exact upstream-authority, Decision-policy, target, and immutable-content binding;
- explicit completed CLEAR, completed HOLD, and evaluation-failed distinctions;
- registered typed/versioned effects with total normalized `type` / `version` / `params` shape;
- safe declared defaults without injecting defaults into external requested-operation constraints;
- RFC 8785/JCS canonicalization plus one LF byte;
- semantic identity over the normalized authority projection while excluding non-authoritative metadata;
- fail-closed behavior for unknown versions, fields, effects, malformed expectations, invalid Unicode, non-interoperable numbers, cycles, excess nesting, and replay substitutions;
- consumer outcomes that stop at `candidate_for_authorization`, `hold`, `evaluation_failed`, `not_applicable`, or `cannot_establish`.

`candidate_for_authorization` is deliberately not operational Authorization or execution permission.

## Evidence lineage

The release is bounded by the RC6 terminal evidence rather than by the desire to make the contract pass. The terminal independent reproduction recorded 166 comparisons, 159 authority-relevant agreements, zero authority-relevant disagreements, and zero public-authority ambiguities. The production candidate subsequently passed the frozen independent-consumer gate, the frozen adversarial apparatus across supported Python lanes, repository regression, and exact Decision Engine producer-to-Apparatus consumption.

Failed and deviating intermediate runs remain preserved in their originating PRs and Actions histories. This release does not erase or relabel them.

## Compatibility and future Contract E hardening

`1.0.0` freezes the currently supported Contract D semantics. Future hardening discovered while Contract E approaches production should not rewrite or move this release.

A Contract E-driven Contract D change may remain in the `1.x` line only when existing valid Contract D 1.0 consumers can continue to interpret the older authority correctly without semantic reinterpretation or a required incompatible shape change. Examples could include separately evidenced compatible additions, stricter non-breaking conformance machinery, or new optional registered capabilities whose absence retains the 1.0 meaning.

If Contract E reveals that an existing Contract D 1.0 field, outcome, applicability rule, identity rule, or required representation must change meaning or become incompatibly shaped, that is not a `1.x` hardening. It requires a new major-version decision and fresh consumer evidence.

The immutable `contract-d-v1.0.0` tag therefore remains the baseline even if later `1.1.0`, `1.2.0`, or a future major version is justified.

## Non-claims

This release does not establish actor identity, approval, delegation, autonomy/trust state, operational Authorization, execution permission, execution occurrence, execution receipts, correctness of upstream epistemic judgments, universal runtime interoperability, unlimited resource behavior, or the final Contract E interface.

No MainFrame mutation or downstream execution is authorized by this release.
