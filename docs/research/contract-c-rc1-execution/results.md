# Contract C RC1 Information-Sufficiency Experiment - Final Report

## Executive result

The **C1 hypothesis survives the frozen synthetic consumer-diversity tests but does not clear the promotion gate**.

Within 18 controlled cases and four materially different destination policies, C1 preserved every policy distinction available from C0. C2 lost most distinctions outside its MainFrame-shaped projection, C3 was unusable as a structured machine authority without parsing conventions, and C0 telemetry was unnecessary for every frozen legitimate decision. Same-verdict controls also showed that a headline verdict alone is insufficient.

However, two high-value gates remain absent: a clean-room independent consumer and a held-out pre-RC1 negative-control replay. In addition, no real production CAL projector emitted the candidate package in this run. Those are not clerical gaps. They test specification sufficiency, contamination risk, and producer realism.

**Final disposition: NEEDS ITERATION**

## 2. Inference

The evidence supports a narrower claim than “C1 is the schema.”

A stable Contract-C seam likely needs a semantic object **richer than a headline verdict or destination projection but materially smaller than CAL's full replay trace**. The stable center is:

1. exact result/input/proposition identity and CAL method identity;
2. retained support, counterevidence, unresolved, and non-deciding contribution references;
3. attributable assessments with explicit unknown/not-performed distinction where behavior differs;
4. CAL conclusion or abstention plus exact basis and blockers;
5. partial/failure/deviation state;
6. reassessment/supersession lineage when applicable;
7. enough semantic measurement receipt state to reconstruct what CAL measured, without automatically stabilizing raw model telemetry.

This is a minimum **capability set**, not a final JSON/YAML schema.

## 3. Provenance and identity result

Current Contract B already has canonical `bundle_id`, schema version, bundle hash, claim IDs, source IDs, passage IDs, and passage hashes. Contract B 1.2.0 explicitly keeps those identities canonical and forbids duplicating their existing text/hash payload into its factual-context extension.

The minimum Contract-C identity scheme supported by this experiment is therefore:

- **CAL run/result set:** immutable `result_set_id` plus content/integrity identity;
- **proposition result:** immutable `result_id` bound to Contract-B `bundle_id` + immutable bundle/tree hash + Contract-B version + canonical `claim_id`/claim hash;
- **evidence contribution:** stable contribution ID referencing canonical Contract-B passage/source IDs and hashes rather than redefining those upstream records;
- **CAL method identity:** CAL/library version plus audit-policy/config hash and any model/rules identity needed to interpret the result;
- **supersession:** newer immutable result points to the result it supersedes; currentness may be derived by an index over immutable results;
- **destination policy identity:** belongs in the downstream decision request/receipt, not in Contract C's CAL result authority.

W3C PROV is useful vocabulary for describing result entities, CAL run activities, agents/operators, derivation, and generation, but the experiment did not demonstrate a need for a general provenance graph runtime.

## 4. Prior-art boundary

Verified against current primary documentation on 2026-08-27:

- OMG DMN 1.5 already standardizes explicit business decision/rule modeling and decision tables: <https://www.omg.org/dmn/>.
- Open Policy Agent already provides structured-input policy evaluation, versioned policy/data bundles, and decision logs that record policy query/input/bundle metadata: <https://www.openpolicyagent.org/docs/management-decision-logs> and <https://www.openpolicyagent.org/docs/management-bundles>.
- W3C PROV already supplies general provenance concepts around entities, activities, agents, and derivation: <https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/>.

**Already solved elsewhere:** generic decision tables, policy-as-code runtime mechanics, policy version/distribution, generic decision logging, generic provenance vocabulary.

**Partly solved:** deterministic downstream policy execution over structured inputs. The current Decision Engine Gate is a small local example, but its generality is not established.

**CAL-specific seam problem:** preserving attributable evidence-relative CAL state, explicit unknowns, counterevidence, assessment state, execution state, and basis across a typed handoff without making the decision system re-audit CAL.

**Genuinely unresolved in RC1:** exact field-level minimum, F3 measurement packaging, clean-room specification reproducibility, portable-vs-reference transport, stable assessment vocabulary, and real producer projection from CAL.

## 5. Remaining hypotheses

1. A compressed C1 with semantic measurement receipts folded into the retained-contribution structure may dominate the current seven-family shape.
2. `not_evaluated` may not need to be an assessment enum if execution-stage receipts can unambiguously distinguish “not run” from “run but unknown.”
3. Reference-oriented C1 may be sufficient if the bound Contract-B artifact is always resolvable; portability duplication has not been shown necessary.
4. A small deterministic Gate/policy runtime may be enough downstream; no evidence here requires generalizing the career select/rank engine.
5. A clean-room consumer may expose hidden specification assumptions not visible in the same-context harness.

## 6. Unresolved unknowns

- Can an independent implementation reproduce the same C1 semantics from specification + frozen artifacts alone?
- Can production CAL project the candidate without opening internals downstream or inventing missing state?
- Do held-out MainFrame incident/fabricated-source cases survive the frozen candidate without schema tuning?
- Which semantic measurement fields are required beyond stable relation/contribution/receipt identity?
- Should “not evaluated” live in per-assessment state, execution state, or both?
- Does any legitimate consumer require portable duplicated passage/claim text rather than immutable Contract-B references?
- How should result-set indexing expose “current” versus “historical” without mutating old immutable results?
- Which CAL confidence constructs, if any, are sufficiently validated to stabilize at Contract C?

## 7. Falsified alternatives

Within the frozen RC1 suite:

1. **C2 as a generally sufficient Contract C** is falsified. It supported 8/72 policy-case probes and none for P2-P4.
2. **C3 human report as canonical machine authority** is falsified under the no-heuristic-parsing criterion. It supported 0/72 structured machine probes.
3. **C0 full implementation telemetry is required downstream** is falsified for P1-P4. Telemetry mutations changed C0 but not C1 or any legitimate decision.
4. **Headline verdict alone is sufficient** is falsified by 19/19 same-verdict residual-state controls.
5. **Destination policy must be embedded in CAL result state** is falsified for the tested P1 context mutation: legitimate decisions changed with identical C1 bytes.
6. **Failed execution can be represented as an ordinary epistemic unknown** is falsified by the partial-run controls: execution failure, completed abstention, and not-started state are behaviorally distinct.

These are bounded falsifications on the frozen suite, not universal impossibility proofs.

## 8. Why this is not `SUPPORTED FOR PROMOTION`

The RC1 branch preregistered clean-room independent-consumer reproduction and a pre-existing negative-control fixture as promotion-critical evidence. Neither was completed. A synthetic same-context harness also cannot establish that the production CAL exporter is lossless or assumption-free.

Calling this promotion-ready would therefore reward the part of the experiment we could run while silently deleting the tests most capable of exposing an underspecified seam. That would violate the project's costly-signaling rule.

## 9. Smallest next evidence-producing iteration

Do **not** redesign C1 broadly. Freeze the current fixture/harness and run three bounded steps:

1. **Clean-room Consumer B:** give only the RC1 logical profile, frozen C1 artifacts, policy specifications, and expected output schema. Forbid CAL implementation and this reference consumer. Compare semantic receipts and document contamination.
2. **Real CAL projector:** on a CAL research branch only, map current production/research CAL result objects into the compressed C1 capability set. Fail if any required field needs an invented default.
3. **Held-out replay:** after the projector and independent consumer are frozen, replay the preserved MainFrame fabricated-source/incident controls without changing their bytes or the Contract-C schema.

If those pass, rerun subfield ablation focused on F2/F3 compression before any Contract-C version/schema decision.
