# NEXT — after Cross-Repository Authority Interface RC2

## Current disposition

The tested evidence supports a research-only Contract E interface candidate.

## Smallest justified specification work

Draft a non-canonical research specification with these separable surfaces:

1. **Common authority envelope**
   - subject/principal/mechanism identity;
   - authority domain;
   - typed operation;
   - exact target identity/currentness;
   - applicability/currentness;
   - receipt/basis reference.

2. **Participant responsibility declaration**
   - responsibilities owned;
   - responsibilities excluded;
   - accepted effect/operation domain;
   - authoritative upstream artifacts;
   - forbidden semantic fields for authority binding.

3. **Domain-specific receipt**
   - semantic validity, source-boundary, access/admission, Decision mandate, or execution authority;
   - domain-local status and reason;
   - no cross-domain implication.

4. **Standing jurisdiction reference**
   - grant/delegation/profile/currentness/revocation;
   - kept distinct from semantic truth.

5. **Enforcement/outcome receipt**
   - what local participant actually did;
   - verification authority kept independently bindable where required.

## Required successor falsifiers before canonical Contract E

- independently reproduced consumer from the research specification;
- stale/revoked authority mutations;
- target currentness mutation;
- cross-version producer conformance;
- missing participant declaration;
- unknown authority domain;
- unknown operation/effect;
- semantic payload byte-identical while authority profile changes;
- authority descriptor byte-identical while semantic payload changes;
- no generic `authorized: true` shortcut.

## Parallel CAL experiment

Run a separate text-to-typed numeric extraction study. Do not fold extraction authority into Contract E merely because both use the word authority.

## Boundary

Do not release Contract E 1.0.0 and do not promote a production Authority Control Plane from RC2.
